Privacy Policy — Candy Girl: Arcade Maze Game

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android game Candy Girl: Arcade Maze Game (package com.ishchenko.candy.girl, "the App") handles information. The App is developed by Artem Ishchenko ("we", "us").

Summary

  • The App does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email or other contact details.
  • The App shows full-screen ads (Google AdMob) between levels, never during a level.
  • The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to collect usage statistics and crash reports that help us improve the game.
  • Game progress stays on your device.
  • We do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

The App saves the following locally on your device only: your level progress and star ratings, candies, lives, items (coffee, candy bombs, snowflakes, fairy dust), shop upgrades, skins, missions, daily challenge progress, tutorial progress and control settings. This data is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC, to show full-screen ads between levels. To select and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • the Android advertising ID and app instance identifiers;
  • device information (device model, operating system version, language) and IP address / approximate location;
  • ad interaction data (ad views and clicks) and diagnostic data.

This data is processed by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) to measure how the game is played: app instance identifiers and, where permitted, the advertising ID; device information (model, OS version, language); approximate location from the IP address (country / region); and gameplay events (levels started, completed, failed or quit, items and upgrades used or bought, missions and daily challenges completed). The App also uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports (stack trace, device model, OS version, app version and the state of the app at the time of the crash). This data is used only to fix errors and improve the App and is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information. Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in our Firebase project (by default up to 14 months).

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above (Google AdMob and Google Firebase).

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: ads, analytics and crash reports.
  • Advertising ID / ad services and Play Install Referrer: used by Google AdMob and Firebase to show and measure ads and installs.
  • Wake lock / foreground service: used by Google libraries to deliver data reliably.

The App does not access your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Data security

All communication between the App and Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not run our own servers and do not store your data ourselves.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help delete it.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Delete all local data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • To request deletion of analytics data associated with your device, contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: artem.ischchenko@gmail.com or indie-mobile-dev.web.app

Политика конфиденциальности — Candy Girl

Последнее обновление: 23 октября 2026 г.

Игра Candy Girl: Arcade Maze Game (com.ishchenko.candy.girl) не требует регистрации и не запрашивает имя, почту или другие контактные данные. Мы не продаём данные.

Прогресс (уровни и звёзды, конфеты, жизни, предметы, улучшения, скины, задания, ежедневное испытание, обучение, настройки управления) хранится только на вашем устройстве и удаляется вместе с данными приложения.

Игра показывает полноэкранную рекламу Google AdMob (Google LLC) между уровнями, никогда во время уровня. Для показа и подсчёта рекламы AdMob может собирать рекламный идентификатор Android, идентификаторы установки, сведения об устройстве, IP-адрес / приблизительное местоположение и данные о взаимодействии с рекламой. В ЕЭЗ, Великобритании и Швейцарии игра запрашивает согласие через форму Google. Рекламный идентификатор можно сбросить или отключить в настройках Android (Настройки → Google → Реклама).

Игра использует Google Analytics for Firebase для статистики (идентификатор установки, сведения об устройстве, страна/регион по IP, игровые события: начало и прохождение уровней, предметы, улучшения, задания) и Firebase Crashlytics для отчётов о сбоях — только чтобы исправлять ошибки и улучшать игру. Данные обрабатываются Google согласно Политике конфиденциальности Google.

Доступа к GPS, контактам, камере, микрофону и файлам у приложения нет. Вопросы и запросы на удаление данных: artem.ischchenko@gmail.com
